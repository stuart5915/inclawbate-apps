💡 Design Tip

Think about the first impression you'd like to make with your business card. A black business card can suggest luxury or power, while a blue business card might inspire feelings of serenity. With a colorful background, you can maintain a minimalist style with your text and imagery, or just go with a classic white for that feeling of professionalism.
