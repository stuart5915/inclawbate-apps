How To Play

Overview

Claw Wars is a strategic trading card game where you command an army of lobster warriors! Reduce your opponent's Life Points from 8000 to 0 to win.

Card Types

  • Monster Cards — Summon creatures to attack and defend. They have ATK (attack) and DEF (defense) values and a Level (stars).
  • Spell Cards — Magical effects: Normal, Continuous, Equip, Quick-Play, Field, and Ritual types.
  • Trap Cards — Set face-down and activate when conditions are met to surprise your opponent.

Turn Phases

  • Draw Phase — Draw 1 card from your deck
  • Main Phase 1 — Summon monsters, activate/set Spells and Traps
  • Battle Phase — Attack with your monsters
  • Main Phase 2 — More summons and card plays
  • End Phase — End your turn

Summoning

  • Level 1-4 monsters: Normal Summon directly (1 per turn)
  • Level 5-6 monsters: Tribute 1 monster you control
  • Level 7+ monsters: Tribute 2 monsters you control
  • Fusion: Use "Polymerization" + listed materials
  • Synchro: Tuner + non-Tuner(s) whose levels equal the Synchro monster's level
  • Ritual: Use a Ritual Spell + tributes matching the level

Battle

  • ATK vs ATK: Lower ATK destroyed, owner takes damage equal to the difference
  • ATK vs DEF: If ATK > DEF, defender destroyed (no damage). If DEF >= ATK, attacker takes damage equal to the difference
  • Direct Attack: If opponent has no monsters, attack their Life Points directly

Rarities

  • Common — Standard card
  • Rare — Silver name text
  • Super Rare — Rainbow shimmer on art
  • Ultra Rare — Gold name + rainbow art
  • Secret Rare — Cross-hatch holographic
  • Ghost Rare — Ethereal ghostly effect
  • Ultimate Rare — Embossed 3D relief
  • Starlight Rare — Prismatic sparkle
